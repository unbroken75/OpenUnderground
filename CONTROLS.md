# Gameplay Controls

The formatted version is online: https://github.com/Kweepa/OpenUnderground/blob/main/CONTROLS.md

Button names use the Xbox layout (A / B / X / Y, LB / RB, LT / RT, Start, Select). In the game, *Button prompts* (in the game menu, under CONTROLS) shows a PlayStation pad's symbols and names instead, and does so by itself when one is connected.

If you use the **Left-handed** option, Attack and Stats swap triggers on the gamepad (see notes under each section).

In the game, the CONTROLS tab of the game menu draws the same keys and buttons on a keyboard and on a pad, and changes them: pick an action, press Enter (A on the pad), then the new key or button. The two swap: the action that had it takes the old one. Keys can be combined with Ctrl, Shift or Alt, and the mouse's middle and side buttons can be bound too. On the keyboard, Delete on a selected action, after a Yes, leaves it with no key, and *Classic keys* sets the whole keyboard to the original game's, from its reference card. The tables below are the defaults.

## GAMEPAD

### Movement \& camera

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Left stick|Move|
|Right stick|Look|
|Left stick click (L3)|Sprint (forward only; uses stamina)|
|A|Jump|

*Automatic jumping* (in the game menu, under CONTROLS): running straight ahead at a gap or a deep drop jumps by itself at the edge. To step off instead, push the stick only partway or to one side.

*Ledge rumble* (in the game menu, under CONTROLS): the pad rumbles briefly when you run at a ledge, a moment before the edge, so you can jump or stop. The row turns off only that rumble.

### Interaction \& combat

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|X|Examine / look at what you are aiming at|
|Y|Use or pick up (when the name turns yellow)|
|B|Cancel a weapon charge, or clear a carried item from the world cursor|
|Right trigger (hold, then release)|Charge attack, then swing|
|B (while charging)|Cancel the charge|

*Left-handed:* use the **left** trigger to charge and swing instead of the right.

### Panels \& menus

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|LB|Open / close magic panel (requires a rune bag)|
|RB|Open / close inventory|
|Left trigger|Show / hide character stats, on the trigger's side|
|Select (Back)|Open / close map (requires a map in your inventory)|
|Start|Open Save / Load / Options (closes open panels first)|
|Start, held|Open the game menu on the gamepad's control map|
|Attack trigger (press)|Also closes open panels|
|D-pad up / down|Scroll back through recent messages (hold to repeat)|

*Message history:* the d-pad is shared with the panels, the map and the quantity prompt, so it
scrolls the log only while none of those are open. On keyboard, Page Up and Page Down work at any
time.

*Left-handed:* use the **right** trigger for stats instead of the left.

### Saving

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Right stick click (R3), hold|Quicksave (five are kept, the sixth replaces the oldest)|

### Sleeping

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|D-pad left, hold|Sleep, on a bedroll if there is one in your pack|

### Track

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|D-pad right|Use the Track skill: the creatures nearby, and in which direction|

### Inventory

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|D-pad|Move selection (stuff list, paperdoll, trade)|
|A (tap)|Look at / use the selected item|
|A (hold)|Pick up the item onto your cursor|
|Y (hold)|Secondary use (equip, eat, and similar)|
|Y (on paperdoll)|Unequip|
|Y (hold), on the rune bag|Open the magic panel|
|X (hold, then release)|Charge a throw (or repair when that applies)|
|B|Back out of an open container|

### Magic panel

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|D-pad|Move rune cursor|
|A|Add the selected rune|
|X|Cast (needs at least two runes)|
|B|Clear the spell|
|D-pad down, from the bottom row|Move onto the active spells; up past the top one goes back to the runes|
|A, on an active spell|Dispel it|
|LB|Close the magic panel|

### Map

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Select|Open / close|
|Left stick|Move the quill|
|A|Place or confirm a note|
|X|Erase the highlighted note|
|D-pad left / right|Previous / next note|
|Left stick, while writing a note|Move the note|
|B (hold)|Reveal fog / extra tiles while held|
|B (release)|Close the map|
|D-pad up / down|Previous / next map page|

### Other

**How many?** (quantity prompts)

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Left stick or D-pad|Adjust the count|
|A|Confirm|
|Y|Confirm alternate (for example, hurl)|
|B|Cancel|

**Flute / instrument**

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Left stick (up / down)|Choose note|
|A|Play note|
|B|Exit|

**Conversations**

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|D-pad up / down|Choose reply|
|A|Confirm|
|Left stick|Scroll the text|

**On-screen keyboard** (names, map notes, and similar)

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|D-pad|Move key cursor|
|Right stick|Move pointer|
|A|Press key|
|Y|Submit / Enter|
|B|Clear|
|X|Cancel (when allowed)|

**Cutscenes**

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|A|Advance, where the A prompt shows; the startup logos and the title screen go to the next|
|B (hold)|Fast forward, ten times speed|
|Start|Leave: the startup logos and the title screen (straight to the title menu), the introduction, the dreams and the two scenes of level 7|

## KEYBOARD \& MOUSE

### Movement \& camera

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|W A S D or Arrow keys|Move|
|Mouse (cursor locked)|Look|
|Left Shift (hold)|Sprint (forward only; uses stamina)|
|Left Shift (hold), with a panel open or a spell waiting for its target|Look with the mouse; a click aims at the crosshair|
|Ctrl (hold)|Walk slowly and quietly|
|Space|Jump|
|Shift + Space|Standing long jump: from standing, a leap forward; on the move, a jump|
|T / G (hold)|Fly up / down, with Fly or Levitate|

Turn left and right, move forward quietly and look down, ahead and up have no key unless you give them one: *Classic keys* puts them on A and D, S, and 1, 2 and 3, as in the original.

Look speed and invert options are in the game menu, under CONTROLS.

*Automatic jumping* (in the game menu, under CONTROLS): running straight ahead at a gap or a deep drop jumps by itself at the edge. To step off instead, hold A or D as well.

### Interaction \& combat

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Left click (short)|Examine / look at what is under the crosshair or free cursor|
|Left click (hold or drag)|Use or pick up (when the name turns yellow)|
|F|Use, or pick up straight into your pack (when the name turns yellow); with an item on the cursor, put it in your pack|
|Right mouse (hold, then release)|Charge attack, then swing|
|X|Cancel a weapon charge|

### Panels \& menus

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Q|Open / close magic panel (requires a rune bag)|
|E|Open / close inventory|
|R|Show / hide character stats, on the right|
|F7|The inventory, then the stats, then closed|
|Tab|Open / close map (requires a map in your inventory)|
|Esc|Close open panels; if none are open, open Save / Load / Options|
|F1|Open the game menu on the control maps (the gamepad's, if you used the pad last)|
|Page Up / Page Down|Scroll back through recent messages (hold to repeat)|

### Saving

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|F5|Quicksave (five are kept, the sixth replaces the oldest)|
|Mouse wheel|Scroll the save and load lists|

### Sleeping

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|F10|Sleep, on a bedroll if there is one in your pack|

### Track

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|F9|Use the Track skill: the creatures nearby, and in which direction|

### Inventory

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Left click|Look / use (short click); drag to pick up; place a carried item|
|Left click (hold outside the panel while carrying)|Charge a throw|
|Right click|Secondary use (equip, eat, unequip, and similar); store into a container when carrying|
|Right click on the rune bag|Open the magic panel|
|F, while carrying|Put the carried item in your pack|
|Scroll wheel|Scroll the stuff list when the pointer is over the grid|

### Magic panel

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Click runes|Build a spell|
|Click the spell runes (or primed cast)|Cast|
|Enter|Start typing runes; opens the magic panel (requires a rune in the rune bag)|
|Letter keys A–W and Y, while typing|Lay a rune on the shelf (Y is Ylem; X and Z are not runes)|
|Space, while typing|Shift the runes left, dropping the first|
|Backspace, while typing|Take back the last rune|
|Enter, while typing|Cast the spell and stop typing|
|Esc, while typing|Stop typing; the runes stay on the shelf and the panel stays open|
|C|Cast the built spell|
|Click the rune shelf, with a panel open|Cast the built spell|
|Click the clear bar|Clear runes|
|Click an active spell, with a panel open|Dispel it|
|Right click or Esc, with a spell waiting for its target|Put the spell away|
|Q, Esc, or right-click outside|Close the panel|

### Map

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Tab or Esc|Open / close|
|Mouse|Move the quill|
|Left click|Place or edit a note|
|Corner arrows|Change map page|

### Other

**How many?** (quantity prompts)

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Mouse (drag arrows)|Adjust the count|
|Right click|Confirm (all / hurl path when that applies)|
|Left click outside the prompt|Take one|
|Esc|Cancel|

**Flute / instrument**

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|0–9 (or keypad)|Play notes|
|Esc|Exit|

**Conversations**

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|1–9|Choose a reply|
|Left or right click|Confirm|

**Text entry** (names, map notes, and similar)

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Type on the keyboard|Enter text|
|Enter|Submit|
|Esc or right-click|Cancel (when allowed)|

**Cutscenes**

|Control|Action|
|-|-|
|Left click|Advance|
|Right click (hold)|Fast forward, ten times speed|
|Esc|Leave: the startup logos and the title screen, the introduction, the dreams and the two scenes of level 7|

The title screen that follows the two startup logos also ends on a left click, Enter or Space, like the
logos themselves, and Esc skips them all, straight to the title menu. Other cutscenes have no outright
skip, only the fast forward.
